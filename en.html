About Raya Loop

A development partner, and a maker of opportunities

Raya Loop is a Saudi establishment specialized in integrated consulting, development and operational solutions. We help organizations reach their goals, make the most of their resources and assets, and turn ideas into projects that deliver value, returns and lasting impact.

Our main focus is developing and empowering the nonprofit sector, and our services extend to government and private-sector partners through one connected system that brings together institutional development, governance, project management, resource development and operations.

We don’t stop at recommending solutions. We work alongside you to make them real.
